# COVENANT — Class System: Roles, not Eras
*A proposed pivot from era-based classes (chronology) to role-based classes (vocation). This is how Hearthstone actually works — you main a fantasy, not a time period — and it lets every icon live in the role that fits.*

---

## The core idea

**Old axis (era):** Beginnings · Deliverance · Judges · Kingdom · Exiles · The Way. These are *chunks of history*. Good for a campaign; weak as an identity you main.

**New axis (role):** the six vocations below. These are *fantasies you pick*, exactly like Warrior/Mage/Priest. Same six-class count, same ~18-cards-each depth, but each class is now a playstyle you identify with — and characters sort by what they did, not when they lived.

**Why it's better:**
- Reads instantly. "I play **Prophet**" tells you the deck (foresight, burn, disruption) the way "I play Mage" does.
- Characters land in the right role. David's sling isn't stuck in a "Kingdom" box; it's a **Shepherd** card.
- Icons can appear in **multiple classes** across sets — three Davids, two Moseses — huge for collection and identity. (Hearthstone does this with alternate heroes and dual-class cards.)
- The campaign is unaffected: roles are orthogonal to the timeline; each chapter just fields whichever classes fit.

---

## The Six Classes

Each owns a **signature mechanic** (à la Hearthstone's Combo/Overload/Outcast), a clear fantasy, and a distinct spot on the "excels/struggles" map.

### 🕮 PROPHET — *foresight & the word that judges*
- **Fantasy:** you see what's coming and call it down.
- **Signature:** **Foresee** (look at the top cards, reorder/bottom) + "fire from heaven" direct damage + disruption (set attack to 1, silence effects, Handwriting on the Wall).
- **Excels at:** card selection, removal, reach, control tempo.
- **Struggles with:** board presence, going wide, sustained pressure.
- **Plays like:** Mage.
- **Bench:** Elijah, Elisha, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Daniel, Nathan, Samuel, Jonah, Hosea, Amos, Micah, Habakkuk, Malachi. *(Deepest single-role bench in Scripture.)*

### ⚔ WARRIOR — *the battle and the blade*
- **Fantasy:** face the giant, take up the weapon, win the field.
- **Signature:** **Relics (weapons/equip)** + giant-slaying (bonus vs high-attack) + combat tricks + Swift.
- **Excels at:** tempo, combat removal, aggression, closing games.
- **Struggles with:** card advantage, healing, the long grind.
- **Plays like:** Warrior / Hunter.
- **Bench:** Joshua, Caleb, David (as king), the Three Mighty Men, Gideon, Samson, Benaiah, Jonathan, Barak, Ehud, Joab, Abner, Abishai. *(Kings and Judges fold in here as sub-themes — or split King out later; see bench options.)*

### ✚ PRIEST — *heal, protect, atone, raise*
- **Fantasy:** guard the people, restore the fallen, keep the flame.
- **Signature:** **Endure** (damage-prevention shields) + healing + **resurrection/atonement** (return fallen allies from the discard).
- **Excels at:** sustain, protection, graveyard value, beating aggression.
- **Struggles with:** proactive damage, closing the game out.
- **Plays like:** Priest.
- **Bench:** Aaron, Eleazar, Phinehas, Melchizedek, Ezra, Eli, Zadok, Jehoiada, the Levites, Samuel (splash). *(Overlaps Prophet on "holy" — split cleanly: Prophet = offense/foresight, Priest = defense/restoration. Classic Mage/Priest divide.)*

### 🐑 SHEPHERD — *not one of them lost*
- **Fantasy:** gather the flock, defend the weak, leave the ninety-nine.
- **Signature:** **Redeem** (when a small ally dies, return it — or summon a Sheep) + Sheep tokens + buffs that scale with how many small units you hold.
- **Excels at:** resilient wide boards, going tall on a wide base, shrugging off removal.
- **Struggles with:** single huge threats, burst damage, racing.
- **Plays like:** Paladin (wide + buff + recursion).
- **Bench:** David (as shepherd boy), Moses (shepherd of Midian), Amos (herdsman-prophet), Jacob, Abel, Rachel, the nativity shepherds, Jabez. *(Distinct from Patriarch: Shepherd protects a wide board **now**; Patriarch ramps to big things **later**.)*
- *Token: Sheep (1/1).*

### ⭑ PATRIARCH — *the covenant that compounds*
- **Fantasy:** plant a promise; watch it become a nation.
- **Signature:** **Covenant** (a card that grows or triggers at the start of each of your turns) + Heir tokens + cost-reduction ramp + permanent +1/+1 accrual.
- **Excels at:** ramp, inevitability, huge late units, long-game value.
- **Struggles with:** early tempo, removal, speed. Punishable by aggro.
- **Plays like:** Druid (ramp) with a touch of Handlock.
- **Bench:** Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Noah, Sarah, Melchizedek, Enoch, Joseph, Job, Methuselah, Shem. *(Distinct from Disciple: Patriarch makes **few, big, growing** things; Disciple makes **many, expendable** ones.)*
- *Token: Heir (1/1, often with a growth rider).*

### 🔥 DISCIPLE — *a spark becomes a wildfire*
- **Fantasy:** send out the many; every loss spreads the fire further.
- **Signature:** **Scatter** (when this dies, summon a Disciple — persecution → multiplication) + Disciple tokens + draw + aggressive buffs.
- **Excels at:** flooding the board, snowballing, converting trades into value, aggression.
- **Struggles with:** board wipes (overcommitment), single big threats, running dry late.
- **Plays like:** Zoo Warlock.
- **Bench:** the Twelve, the Seventy, Stephen, Silas, Barnabas, Philip, Timothy, Titus, the deacons, Cornelius, Lydia, Apollos.
- *Token: Disciple (1/1).*

## The "wide trio" problem, solved

Three of your six lean toward tokens. Here's the razor that keeps them distinct — the exact same three axes Hearthstone runs as separate decks:

| Class | Board shape | Timing | Attitude to its units | HS parallel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Patriarch** | few, **big**, growing | late-game | precious — protect and grow them | Ramp Druid |
| **Shepherd** | many, **small**, protected | midrange | keep them all alive (Redeem) | Paladin |
| **Disciple** | many, **expendable**, multiplying | early/aggressive | spend them; death spreads | Zoo Warlock |

No two want the same thing. That's a healthy meta triangle, not redundancy.

---

## The big unlock: characters across multiple classes

Sorting by role means the icons appear where they served — and the giants of Scripture appear in **several** classes across the game's life. Each is a different card, different art, different fantasy:

- **David** → Shepherd (the boy & sling), Warrior (the king), a Prophet nod (the psalmist).
- **Moses** → Prophet (signs & judgment), Patriarch (the lawgiver, father of a people), Shepherd (of Midian).
- **Samuel** → Prophet (the seer) and Priest (raised at the tabernacle).
- **Elijah** → Prophet (fire) with a Warrior-adjacent showdown on Carmel.
- **Aaron** → Priest (the obvious home), Warrior-adjacent (the rod).

This is a *collection* dream: chase all three Davids. It's also a *teaching* dream: a player literally sees that one life filled many callings. Hearthstone would kill for lore this rich to justify multi-class cards.

## The campaign still works

Roles are orthogonal to the timeline, so the gospel arc is unaffected — each chapter just fields the classes that fit its moment:

- *Out of Egypt* → Prophet + Shepherd (Moses) vs the Adversary.
- *Giants in the Land* → Warrior (David) vs the Philistine host.
- *By the Rivers of Babylon* → Priest + Prophet (Daniel, the Three) endurance fight.
- *The Way Forward* → Disciple + Shepherd (the early church) vs persecution.

Same structure as the GDD; the Cross/Resurrection interlude and the Jesus design-law are untouched.

## Bench options — swap any of the six if you want a different flavor

Your six are the recommended set. But here's the fuller menu, in case you'd rather trade one in:

- **King** *(swap for Warrior)* — anthems, thrones, decrees, royal Relics/crowns, dynasty snowball. A more distinctive fantasy than generic "Warrior," and Warrior's combat cards could fold under it. Bench: Saul, David, Solomon, Hezekiah, Josiah, Uzziah.
- **Scribe / Teacher** — the Law and knowledge: card draw, spell tutoring, combo enablers. A value/combo class. Bench: Ezra, Baruch, Gamaliel, Apollos, the scribes. *(A bit dry as a fantasy; strong mechanically.)*
- **Judge** — the underdog: comeback-when-behind, Spirit-empowerment burst, sacrifice. Bench: Othniel–Samson, Deborah, Samuel. *(Aggro/tempo; could be a Warrior sub-theme instead.)*
- **Queen / Matriarch** — clutch and reactive: "for such a time" effects that trigger off being hit, hidden power, sacrifice for the people. Bench: Esther, Deborah, Ruth, Abigail, Jael, Sarah. *(A genuinely novel reactive class — no clean Hearthstone parallel, which could be a feature.)*
