| D-38 | **A stronger AI to test against — `pickActionStrong` (2-ply rollout policy).** The greedy AI (`pickAction`) judges each move by the board it leaves *right now* — zero opponent modeling — which is why tempo/aggro (Warrior) flatters it. The new policy does **one-step rollout improvement with a 2-ply horizon**: for each candidate opening move it finishes the turn greedily, simulates the **opponent's whole reply**, and scores the position at the start of its next turn (so defense/trades are priced correctly). Deterministic (greedy rollouts, no RNG). New harness `tools/ai-eval.ts`: **(A)** strong vs greedy, mirror decks, both seats → **strong won 88.6%** (decisively stronger); **(B)** class spread greedy-both (baseline **50 pts**: Warrior 83% → Prophet 33%) vs strong-both (*number pending — the strong-both matrix is the compute-heavy run; will record the spread + whether Warrior's lead is an AI artifact or real*).
