# 08 — Card Additions (Set 1 Completion)
*Supplement to `04-card-database.md`. Does not replace it — these are the fill cards that bring Set 1 from ~97 to a complete **120 collectible**, plus the finished **Leaders + Hero Powers** (12) and the expanded **Adversary pool** (~24). Same conventions: Class + Tag, stat rail atk+hp ≈ cost×2+1, keywords ≈ −1. Flavor rule holds: joke about the humans, never the holy. Every card carries a 🎨 art seed. New IDs must not collide with those in `04`.*

## Fill: ✚ PRIEST (+6 → 18 total)

🎨 A censer trailing blue smoke over a golden altar, curling script of incense, hush.

**Phinehas the Zealous** — 3 · 3/2 · Rare · `Exodus`

🎨 A priest striding forward with a spear, censer smoking at his belt, resolve, incense-blue and gold.

Whenever you restore health, restore 1 more. *"Backed the right king when it counted. Anointed Solomon. — 1 Kings 1."*
🎨 A dignified high priest with an anointing horn, temple pillars behind, calm authority.

**Day of Atonement** — 4 · Spell · Epic · `Exodus`
Restore your hero to full health. *"Once a year, the slate wiped clean. The whole point, in one day. — Leviticus 16."*
🎨 A lone goat walking toward a bright wilderness horizon, camp receding, weight visibly lifting.

## Leaders & Hero Powers (2 per class)
*A Leader is the hero portrait you pick — it sets your class and grants a Hero Power (2 Provision, once per turn, deliberately weaker than any 2-drop). Some share a name with a collectible card; the Leader is the class avatar, a distinct entity from the card.*

**🕮 Prophet**
- **Isaiah** — *Vision (2):* Foresee 2. 🎨 A seer with coal-touched lips beneath seraph-light.
- **Elijah** — *Fire (2):* Deal 1 damage to a unit. 🎨 A windswept prophet, a spark leaping from his hand.

**⚔ Warrior**
- **Joshua** — *Rally (2):* Give a friendly unit +1 attack this turn. 🎨 A captain raising a horn before a marching column.
- **Deborah** — *Command (2):* Give a friendly unit +1/+1 this turn. 🎨 A judge enthroned beneath a palm, warriors attending.

**✚ Priest**
- **Aaron** — *Intercede (2):* Restore 2 health to your hero. 🎨 A jeweled breastplate, censer raised, incense-blue.
- **Melchizedek** — *Consecrate (2):* Give a friendly unit Endure. 🎨 Bread and cup offered on a hilltop, face in shadow.

**🐑 Shepherd**
- **David** — *Gather (2):* Summon a 1/1 Sheep. 🎨 A shepherd boy with harp and sling at dawn.
- **Moses** — *Lead (2):* Give a friendly unit +0/+2. 🎨 A herdsman before an unconsuming flame, staff in hand.

**⭑ Patriarch**
- **Abraham** — *Multiply (2):* Summon a 1/1 Heir. 🎨 An elder beneath an overwhelming field of stars.
- **Sarah** — *Promise (2):* Give a friendly unit +1/+1. 🎨 A tent door, a knowing laugh, three long shadows.

**🔥 Disciple**

- **Paul** — *Exhort (2):* Give a friendly Disciple +1/+1. 🎨 A prisoner writing by lamplight, chains at his ankle.

## Adversary Pool — completion (~24, AI-only, never collectible)
*Players never own these. Used by campaign & skirmish AI. Extends the 9 already noted in `04`. Bosses are oversized on purpose.*

Already defined (in `04`): Goliath of Gath, Egyptian Taskmaster, Philistine Soldier, Amalekite Raider, Chariot of Iron, Jezebel, Pharaoh's Magician, Idol of Ba'al, The Serpent.

**New Adversary units**
- **Pharaoh, the Hardened** — 7 · 6/8 · Boss · Arrival: your enemy's units can't attack next turn (his heart hardens). 🎨 An enthroned king of Egypt, arms crossed, cold gold, a river of shadow behind.
- **Haman the Agagite** — 5 · 5/4 · Boss · Legacy: the gallows he built fall on the strongest enemy unit. 🎨 A sneering vizier beside an enormous empty gallows, torchlight, dread irony.
- **Nebuchadnezzar** — 8 · 7/9 · Boss · Arrival: bow or burn — deal 3 to all enemy units that don't have Guard. 🎨 A colossal golden statue with a king enthroned before it, furnace-glow.
- **Herod** — 6 · 5/6 · Boss · Arrival: destroy the enemy's cheapest unit. 🎨 A paranoid king on a too-large throne, soldiers in shadow, cold marble.
- **Sennacherib's Host** — 5 · 5/5 · Arrival: summon two 2/2 Assyrian Soldiers. 🎨 A vast siege camp bristling with spears outside a walled city at dusk.
- **Delilah** — 3 · 2/3 · Arrival: an enemy unit loses its keywords and gets −2 attack. 🎨 An elegant figure with shears, a sleeping strongman's silhouette, candlelit betrayal.
- **Absalom** — 4 · 5/3 · Swift · Legacy: deal 2 to the enemy hero. 🎨 A proud prince on a mule beneath a great oak, long hair, storm light.
- **Sisera** — 4 · 4/4 · Guard · 🎨 An iron-chariot commander surveying a plain, bronze and cruelty.
- **The Witch of Endor** — 3 · 2/3 · Arrival: return a fallen Adversary from the discard. 🎨 A dim cave, a hooded figure over a guttering fire, cold blue smoke.
- **Sanballat & Tobiah** — 3 · 3/3 · Arrival: an enemy relic costs 2 more to activate. 🎨 Two mocking officials pointing at a half-built wall, sneers, dusty ridge.
- **Leviathan** — 8 · 8/8 · Boss · Guard · takes no damage from spells. 🎨 A vast sea-serpent coiling through black water, moonlight on scales — awe, not gore. — Job 41.
- **Behemoth** — 7 · 6/8 · Boss · Endure · 🎨 An enormous beast at a marsh's edge, cedar-strong, dawn mist. — Job 40.
- **The Golden Calf** — 3 · Relic (standing) · At the end of the AI's turn, give a random Adversary +1/+1. 🎨 A gilded calf idol on a pedestal, revelers' shadows, firelight.

*Adversary tokens: Assyrian Soldier (2/2), Locust (1/1 Swift).*
