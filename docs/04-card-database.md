# COVENANT — Set 1 "Promised Land" · Master Card Database
*Every card re-filed with two fields: **Class** (role — your deck's identity, like Warrior/Mage) and **Tag** (era — a cross-cutting tribe, like Murloc/Dragon). ~120 collectible cards. Stat rail: atk+hp ≈ cost×2+1; keywords ≈ −1. Flavor rule: joke about the humans, never the holy.*

---

## How to read a card
> **Name** — cost · attack/health · Rarity · `Tag` · Keywords
> Rules text. *"Flavor line. — Passage."*
> 🎨 Art direction (subject · pose · setting · mood).

## The six Classes & their signature mechanic
- 🕮 **Prophet** — **Foresee** (peek/reorder your deck) + judgment burn. *Sees it coming, calls it down.*
- ⚔ **Warrior** — **Relics** (equip weapons) + giant-slaying. *The battle and the blade.*
- ✚ **Priest** — **Endure** (damage-prevention shield) + heal + **Raise** (return the fallen). *Guard, restore, atone.*
- 🐑 **Shepherd** — **Redeem** (a small ally that dies comes back / becomes a Sheep). *Not one lost.*
- ⭑ **Patriarch** — **Covenant** (triggers at the start of each of your turns) + ramp. *The promise that compounds.*
- 🔥 **Disciple** — **Scatter** (when this dies, summon a Disciple) + swarm. *A spark becomes wildfire.*

## The six Tags (eras, cross-cutting)
`Genesis` · `Exodus` · `Judges` · `Kingdom` · `Exile` · `Apostles`
Tags enable payoff cards like *"Your Exodus cards cost (1) less"* and keep story-teams synergistic even when split across classes. Neutrals are untagged.

## Art direction — global
Illuminated-manuscript-meets-modern-epic. Painterly, cinematic, gold-leaf accents on parchment-and-ink; deep shadow, warm light. Reverent and grounded — **no cartoon halos, no kitsch, no visible face of God.** One saturated signature color per class:
- Prophet = storm-blue & fire-orange · Warrior = bronze & scarlet · Priest = white-gold & incense-blue · Shepherd = dawn-green & wool-cream · Patriarch = starfield-indigo & desert-tan · Disciple = flame-red & amber.
